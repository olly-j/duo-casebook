About CASEBOOK

CASEBOOK is a source-led iPhone Duo case catalogue published as CASEBOOK and maintained by olly-j.

How the catalogue is made

AI-assisted research brings together publicly available manufacturer pages, retailer listings, announcements and clearly identified previous-model review evidence. Each case links to its product sources and records the research date. Source-published photographs or renders retain their attribution and any representation caveats.

CASEBOOK has not independently handled or drop-tested these Duo cases. Manufacturer descriptions are claims by those sources. Previous-model reviews remain tied to the exact model tested. The catalogue assigns no numeric brand rating or product review score.

What the records mean

Unknown facts remain unknown. Price currencies are preserved. Listing status reflects the checked page, not live inventory. Shipping, warranty and return terms can depend on the seller and selected variant. Confirm them before buying.

Decorative variants may be grouped when they share a design. Unnamed future lineups without verifiable case visuals belong in the coverage registry, rather than the product grid. Public discovery cannot guarantee every seller or unannounced product. No automatic monitoring is running.

Corrections and transparency

Report a correction through GitHub Issues. Include the case name, source URL and the fact that needs checking. Avoid including personal order details in a public issue.

CASEBOOK is an independent catalogue with no affiliation to Apple or the case manufacturers. Source code and research records are available in the project repository. Advertising is currently off; any future activation requires publisher setup and certified consent configuration. Read the privacy information.
