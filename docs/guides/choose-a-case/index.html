Buying guide

Choose coverage before comparing price or weight

Start with the parts of the phone you want covered. Then compare materials, measurements and delivery terms within that group.

Front, back and hinge are separate choices

A front frame protects a different area from a back shell. A low price or weight for one component does not describe a complete set. A two-part case can cover front and back while leaving the hinge exposed.

A flexible hinge film is not a structural hinge cover. Check the recorded hinge field and the source photographs. A folio flap also needs its own coverage check; its name alone does not establish protection around every edge.

Read the material, then the construction

Aramid fibre is a material description, not a drop-test result. A thin aramid back can leave the front and hinge uncovered. Compare the complete assembly, including frames, glass and any separate parts.

TPU and polycarbonate describe different plastics used in flexible sections and rigid shells. Neither label establishes corner protection, long-term discolouration or survival in a fall. Source-published drop claims do not form a comparable laboratory ranking.

Leather, synthetic leather and technical fabric are separate materials. A leather-like product name can describe a synthetic finish. Read material notes and limitations before choosing by feel or appearance. Previous-model wear evidence applies only to the named material and model tested.

Keep measurements in context

Case weight is not package weight or magnetic holding force. Wall thickness is not the depth of a closed phone with its case fitted. A front-only component cannot be compared fairly with a full set on weight alone.

Unknown measurements stay unknown. They are not zero and cannot prove that a case is lighter or thinner. Read the measurement notes for tolerances, selected variants and what was actually measured.

Check price and UK delivery separately

Prices retain the seller’s currency. A USD price and a GBP price are not directly comparable numbers. A UK listing does not establish live stock, UK delivery, tax, import duty or a final delivered total.

Preorders, coming-soon pages and source-published concepts have different purchase implications. Read the dated status and dispatch note, then confirm the selected colour and model at the seller before paying.

Use sorting as a shortlist tool

Preference matching uses published attributes. It is not an independent quality score or a product endorsement. Historical brand reviews offer context for the exact older case tested; they do not demonstrate Duo performance.

Choose two to four cases with comparable coverage in the catalogue. Check their limitations, colour options, source dates and seller pages together. If a missing dimension matters to your pocket or grip, ask the seller rather than estimating from a photograph.
