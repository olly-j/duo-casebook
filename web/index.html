Sources and method

What the sources tell us

Advertising is currently off. Any future ads will be labelled and kept separate from case order and preference matches.

Official sources describe manufacturer claims, not independent testing. Retailer listings and announcements are identified separately. Previous-model hands-on evidence does not establish performance of an untested Duo case.

Vendor drop-height claims are not comparable laboratory rankings. A UK listing price is not a promised delivered total. Source currencies are never converted.

This is a dated snapshot of public discovery, not exhaustive coverage or access to unannounced products. No automatic refresh is running. Saved cases stay in your browser.

Brand coverage

Each case shows its source type and availability. Brand research may cover older phone models; it does not establish Duo performance. Neither source type nor brand history is a quality score.
